# Privacy Policy / Политика конфиденциальности

**shorts-factory** — automated service that generates and publishes short historical videos to YouTube.

## What data we use
- The service accesses the YouTube account of the channel owner only to upload videos, using Google OAuth 2.0 (scope: youtube.upload).
- No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with third parties.
- No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

## Data usage
- OAuth tokens are stored privately in the GitHub repository secrets and used solely for automated video uploads to the owner's own channel.
