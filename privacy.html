Privacy Policy

shorts-factory is an automated service that generates and publishes short historical videos to YouTube.

What data we use

  • The service accesses the YouTube account of the channel owner only to upload videos, using Google OAuth 2.0 (scope: youtube.upload).
  • No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with third parties.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.

Data usage

OAuth tokens are stored privately in GitHub repository secrets and are used solely for automated video uploads to the owner's own channel.
